If you missed the [launch post](/blog/introducing-lookma/): you talk to your Mac, and an agent does
the work in your real apps, on your own screen, while you watch. This is what changed in the two
days since.

## Pick your agent

```sh
lookma ~/Projects                  # Claude Code, as before
lookma --agent codex ~/Projects    # Codex CLI, on your ChatGPT plan
```

**Codex** runs through its app server. Its commands stay in Codex's own sandbox: they may write only
in the folders you pass, with no network, and anything more comes to you as a card.

**Claude Code** no longer lives in a terminal. {NAME} now runs your own installed `claude` headless,
through the Claude Agent SDK, still on your Claude plan. That removed the part of {NAME} I liked
least: reading a terminal once a second and answering its dialogs with arrow keys. Approvals are
now real requests, the warning card at every start is gone, and "stop" stops: the work, anything
you added just before it, and every card it had open.

## Both ends are plug-ins

	name: 'plug-ins',

An agent is one folder in `executors/`, a voice one folder in `voices/`. Each says what settings it
needs in a small manifest, and {NAME} loads it, checks it, and owns its lifecycle: status, the queue
of requests, stopping, and one restart if the agent dies. An agent adapter only translates, with
four methods: start, prompt, interrupt, close.

Whatever is plugged in, the agent touches the screen only through {NAME}'s six hands tools, so every
step stays visible, asked for and stoppable. Adding Gemini CLI, Aider or a second voice should be a
folder and a page of docs. The guides are in the repo:
[writing an executor](https://github.com/liuyoshio/lookma/blob/main/docs/writing-an-executor.md) and
[writing a voice](https://github.com/liuyoshio/lookma/blob/main/docs/writing-a-voice.md).

## A voice that talks back

The first voice was a terse butler: one sentence or nothing, silent while it worked. In daily use
that felt like talking to a machine. I rebuilt it around one idea: it owes you things, a reply to
everything you say and every update from the work, and a debt is paid only when you actually heard
a true answer.

- **It answers when you finish talking.** It no longer waits for a transcript first.
- **It says what it is doing.** "OK, opening Maps", then quiet until there is news.
- **It decides what was meant for it.** A cough, the TV, people talking: it stays silent.
- **Nothing gets lost.** An update cut off by a dropped connection, a pause or a reload is said
  afterwards.
- **With headphones you can cut in**, like with a person. Through speakers it tries to finish its
  sentence first, though its own echo can still get in the way. Headphones work best.

The spoken "yes, I approve" still has a second, independent check in code. That part did not
change.

## Settings

Every setting now comes from a flag, the environment or `.env`, in that order, and `lookma config`
tells you what is in effect and where it came from.

```sh
lookma --agent codex --audio headset ~/Projects
lookma config
```

## Try it

```sh
git pull && npm install
lookma doctor
```
